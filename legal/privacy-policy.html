Privacy Policy

Last updated: February 19, 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy describes how Available ApS ("Available", "we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, and protects information when you use our Zendesk Marketplace applications ("Apps"). Available is committed to protecting your privacy and handling your data responsibly.

2. Information We Collect

Our Apps may collect and process the following types of information:

  • Zendesk Account Information: Your Zendesk subdomain, agent email address, and agent name — used to authenticate and scope your session.
  • Third-Party OAuth Tokens: When you connect a third-party service (e.g., ClickUp), we store the OAuth access token to make API calls on your behalf.
  • Task and Ticket Associations: We store the mapping between Zendesk tickets and third-party tasks (e.g., ClickUp task IDs) to maintain linked data.
  • Organization Mappings: Configuration data that maps Zendesk organizations to third-party project structures.
  • Time Entries: Time tracking data is sent directly to the third-party service (e.g., ClickUp) and is not stored on our servers beyond the active session.

3. How We Use Information

We use collected information solely to:

  • Provide and operate the App's functionality within Zendesk.
  • Authenticate your identity and authorize third-party API access.
  • Maintain the link between Zendesk tickets and third-party resources.
  • Improve and troubleshoot our Apps.

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.

4. Data Storage and Security

Data is stored using Convex, a cloud database platform with encryption in transit (TLS) and at rest. Access tokens are stored securely and scoped per Zendesk subdomain and agent. We implement reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your data.

5. Third-Party Services

Our Apps integrate with third-party services such as ClickUp. When you connect these services, their own privacy policies and terms of service also apply. We encourage you to review the privacy policies of any third-party services you connect.

6. Data Retention

We retain your data for as long as the App is installed and active on your Zendesk account. If you uninstall the App or disconnect a third-party service, associated OAuth tokens are deleted. You may request deletion of all your data by contacting us.

7. Your Rights

Under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), you have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you.
  • Request correction or deletion of your data.
  • Withdraw consent for data processing at any time.
  • Lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority.

8. Cookies

Our Apps do not use cookies. Authentication is handled via the Zendesk App Framework (ZAF) SDK and OAuth tokens.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify users of significant changes by updating the "Last updated" date above.
